Understanding the Model

This model projects membership and revenue over time based on a set of assumptions you can control. It's designed to help you understand the financial dynamics of your membership model and the potential impacts of changes in pricing, recruitment, and retention.

Key Concepts

  • Legacy Members: A distinct group of existing members with their own price and attrition rate. You can model a gradual price increase for this group.
  • New Members & Cohorts: New members are acquired each month. For visualization, they are grouped into annual cohorts. The price for new members can increase annually with inflation.
  • Attrition: The percentage of members who leave each month. The model uses a smoothed attrition curve, interpolating between the yearly rates for a more realistic projection.
  • Price Sensitivity: An optional feature to model the impact of price changes on recruitment and retention. When enabled, price increases will reduce the number of new members and increase the attrition rate.
  • Lifetime Value (LTV): A projection of the total revenue a new member will generate over their entire time as a member. It is calculated based on the initial price, annual price increases, and the smoothed, tenure-based attrition rates.

How to Use the Chart

Use the controls to adjust the parameters of the model. The chart will update automatically to show the projected impact. The stacked area chart shows the composition of your membership over time. The line charts on the right axis show the projected monthly revenue and the price for new members.

  • Use the "Base New Members per Month" slider to see how changes in recruitment affect growth. If price sensitivity is enabled, this number will be adjusted based on the price.
  • Adjust the attrition rates to understand the impact of improved (or declining) retention. Notice how small changes in early-year attrition can significantly impact LTV and long-term revenue.
